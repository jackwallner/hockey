Hockey Edge is an NHL advanced stats app for iPhone. It ranks every skater and goalie by expected goals, shows each player's percentile against their own position, and gives each game an advanced box score. The current season is free, with no account and no ads.

PLAYERS AND LEADERBOARDS
• Percentile bars for every metric, ranked among forwards, among defensemen or among goalies
• Advanced metrics include ixG, goals above expected (GAx), xGF%, CF%, HDCF%, and for goalies GSAx and high-danger save percentage
• Standard stats too: points, goals, assists, plus/minus, ice time, hits, blocks and faceoff percentage, and for goalies SV%, GAA, wins and shutouts
• Leaderboards by position, sorted by any stat, with filters for East or West, rookies and a playing-time minimum
• Search players and teams by name
• Player pages with Advanced and Standard tabs, a game log, and a share button that makes an image of the percentile card
• A glossary that explains every stat

GAMES
• Each night's slate with live scores that refresh every thirty seconds during games
• Advanced box score for each final: expected goals, shot attempts, high-danger chances, goals above expected, and a cumulative xG race by period
• Upcoming games show a win chance, records, last five games, power play, penalty kill and power ratings
• Expected goals and player stats usually follow the final by the next morning

TEAMS
• A page for all 32 clubs with advanced stats, standard stats and a roster you can rank by any metric
• Division standings, East and West
• Power ratings that rank the league by goals better or worse than an average team, adjusted for schedule
• Follow a team and its game is pinned to the top of the slate

FOLLOWING
• Follow players and see their season stats together on one list

FREE AND STATSCOUT+
The current season is free, regular season and playoffs, with everything above, and it does not expire. StatScout+ is an optional upgrade, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions include a free trial for eligible new subscribers, and the app shows its length before you start. StatScout+ adds:
• Trends: the league ranked by who is heating up and cooling off
• Form over the last 2, 4 or 8 weeks for players and teams
• Compare two players, two teams, or one player across seasons
• Every season back to 2008-09, plus all-time career percentiles
• Every player's line in each box score, with ixG, GAx and goalie GSAx
• Best & Worst: the league leader and trailer on every metric

Prices appear in the app before you buy and vary by region. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

PRIVACY
• No account, no ads and no cross-app tracking
• The players and team you follow are stored on your iPhone
• The app makes network requests for stats, live scores and purchases

Expected goals and shot data are from MoneyPuck.com. Schedules, box scores and player bios are public NHL data.
