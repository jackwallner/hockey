See who leads beyond the box score.
Rank NHL players by expected goals, read a full percentile profile in one tap, and track who is heating up or cooling off right now.

StatScout puts ixG, goals above expected, xGF%, Corsi, high-danger chances, goals saved above expected, and the familiar box-score totals in one fast iPhone app.

VISUAL PERCENTILES
Every metric displays as a horizontal percentile bar with a colored marker, the same read fans know from NHL EDGE: blue is cold, red is hot, and the league's elite jump off the page. Forwards rank against forwards, defensemen against defensemen, goalies against goalies.

COMPLETE PLAYER PROFILES
Tap any player to see the full picture: percentile rankings across Scoring, Shot Quality, Play Driving and Goaltending, a switch between advanced metrics and standard season stats, and recent form game by game.

STATS THAT KEEP UP WITH GAME NIGHT
During the season, StatScout checks for newly published numbers after every night's games and updates as soon as they arrive. Every screen shows which games the numbers include, so you always know what you are looking at.

GAMES AND TEAMS
Every night's slate with final scores, an advanced box score for each game (expected goals, shot attempts, high-danger chances and the cumulative xG race), standings, team power ratings and projected margins.

LEADERBOARDS AND FOLLOWING
Start on familiar leaders like points and goals, or switch to advanced rankings. Filter by Forwards, Defensemen or Goalies, and search every active player without losing league rank. Keep your team and favorite players together in Following, free. Tap any row to open the full profile.
