See who leads beyond the box score.
Rank NFL players by advanced metrics, read a full percentile profile in one tap, and track who is heating up or cooling off right now.

StatScout puts EPA per play, CPOE, YAC over expected, rushing efficiency, receiving production, defensive impact, and the familiar box-score totals in one fast iPhone app.

VISUAL PERCENTILES
Every metric displays as a horizontal percentile bar with a colored marker. The color shifts continuously from rust (0th percentile) through sand (50th) to turf green (100th), so you can spot elite performers instantly.

COMPLETE PLAYER PROFILES
Tap any player to see the full picture: percentile rankings across Passing, Rushing, Receiving, and Defense, a switch between advanced metrics and standard season stats, and recent form game by game.

STATS THAT KEEP UP WITH GAME DAY
During the season, StatScout checks for newly published NFL stats throughout the day and updates as soon as they arrive. Every screen shows which week and games the numbers include, so you always know what you are looking at.

LEADERBOARDS AND FOLLOWING
Start on familiar leaders like passing yards, or switch to advanced rankings. Filter by Passing, Rushing, Receiving, or Defense, and search every active player without losing league rank. Keep your team and favorite players together in Following, free. Tap any row to open the full profile.

STATSCOUT+
Unlock the rest of the app with StatScout+:
- Trends: the whole league ranked by who is heating up and cooling off
- Recent Form: last 3 / 5 / 8 games on any player, club or leaderboard
- Player Comparisons: any two players side by side, or one player year over year
- Team Scouting: advanced and standard stats for all 32 clubs
- Historical Seasons: every year back to 2000, plus All-Time career percentiles

Choose Monthly, Yearly (with free trial), or Lifetime. One purchase covers everything.

PRIVACY-FIRST
No accounts. No sign-up. No ads. No third-party tracking. The app makes only the network requests needed for NFL statistics and optional purchase verification.
