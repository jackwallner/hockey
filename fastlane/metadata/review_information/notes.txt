The App Store name is Football Next: StatScout. The app is named Gridiron StatScout in-app and StatScout on the Home Screen. It is a read-only NFL statistics viewer with no accounts or sign-in.

Core flow:
1. Launch the app and open the Stats tab for current-season player rankings.
2. Filter by position, choose a statistic, and open a player profile for percentile rankings, standard stats, bio, injury status, contract value, and game log.
3. Open Games for the schedule, posted scores, box scores, and advanced EPA breakdowns.
4. Open Teams for records, standings, team power ratings, and projected margins.
5. Contract Value is available in player profiles and on the Stats board. The top-ranked player is free; the full board is part of StatScout+.
6. Following, player comparisons, Trends, Recent Form, and historical views are available from their tabs or player/team pages.

StatScout+ features (IAP via RevenueCat):
- Trends, Recent Form, player comparisons, team scouting, historical seasons back to 2000, and All-Time career percentiles
- Restore Purchases is available in Settings; purchases can be tested with an Apple sandbox account

Data: public nflverse / nflreadpy NFL statistics, refreshed nightly. Not affiliated with the NFL, any NFL team, or the NFLPA. No league, team, or player imagery is used.

The app has no accounts, social features, live scores, play-by-play, betting odds, wagers, or fantasy team management. Game projections are informational estimates, not betting odds.
