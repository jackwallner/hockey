## Hockey Edge: Puck StatScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Hockey Edge: Puck StatScout is a read-only NHL statistics app. It does not
require an account.

## Data used by the app

The app fetches publicly available NHL statistics (expected goals and shot data from MoneyPuck.com, schedule and box scores from the NHL) from a Supabase-hosted
database. Network requests necessarily include technical information
such as an IP address, request time, user agent, and response status. We
use that information for delivery, security, and reliability. We do not
use it to build a profile or track you across apps.

Your filters, selected seasons, and other app preferences are stored
locally on your device. The app does not collect your name, email,
contacts, photos, location, health data, search history, or player-view
history. It does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Purchases and subscriptions

StatScout+ includes monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a
one-time lifetime purchase. Monthly and yearly plans may include a 7-day
introductory free trial for eligible accounts, as shown by Apple at
purchase. Apple processes payment. RevenueCat verifies purchases and
entitlement status and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction
and entitlement information, and limited technical app or device
context. It does not receive your hockey activity or personal contact
information. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Local
preferences remain until you delete them or remove the app. Supabase and
Apple may retain technical and transaction records under their own
policies. Contact us at the address below for a privacy request.

## Children

Hockey Edge: Puck StatScout is not directed to children under 13 and does
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date
above identifies the current version.
