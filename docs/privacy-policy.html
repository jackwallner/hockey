Football Next: StatScout Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Football Next: StatScout is a read-only NFL statistics app. It does not require an account.

Data used by the app

The app fetches publicly available NFL statistics from a Supabase-hosted database. Network requests necessarily include technical information such as an IP address, request time, user agent, and response status. We use that information for delivery, security, and reliability. We do not use it to build a profile or track you across apps.

Your filters, selected seasons, and other app preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, location, health data, search history, or player-view history. It does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Purchases and subscriptions

StatScout+ includes monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Monthly and yearly plans may include a 7-day introductory free trial for eligible accounts, as shown by Apple at purchase. Apple processes payment. RevenueCat verifies purchases and entitlement status and may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and entitlement information, and limited technical app or device context. It does not receive your football activity or personal contact information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. Local preferences remain until you delete them or remove the app. Supabase and Apple may retain technical and transaction records under their own policies. Contact us at the address below for a privacy request.

Children

Football Next: StatScout is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The date above identifies the current version.
