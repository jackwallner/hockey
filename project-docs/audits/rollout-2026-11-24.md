## Star prompt: how it was before

Two triggers called `requestReview()` through `RootTabView`:

- A third or later player profile open (`PlayerProfileView.onAppear`). Not a good result: an open count. Removed.
- A Pro member's `PlayerComparisonView.onAppear`. A view appearing, with no deliberate result behind it. Tightened (below).

The 3 second delay, the sheet check and the once-per-session guard lived in `RootTabView` and were driven by a notification and a stored "pending" flag. That is replaced by the modifier, so the view that raised the moment is the one re-checked.

## Good moments chosen

1. Following a third player (`PlayerProfileView`, the star button). The watchlist is what the Trends tab reads, and three followed players means the person built something they came back for. Only a follow counts, never an unfollow.
2. A StatScout+ member opening a head-to-head comparison they built (`PlayerComparisonView`, first appearance after they picked both players). The person already paid and is using the feature they paid for. Free users never trigger it, so no ask follows a paywall or trial sheet.

This app has no scored result, so these are the clearest positive actions it has. Never on launch, in onboarding, in the first session (tracker needs the 2nd launch), after an error, or after a paywall or purchase sheet (any presented sheet blocks the ask).

## Gates, unchanged

2nd cold launch or later, onboarding done, 30-day cooldown stamped when the call is made, opening the write-review page ends the passive asks, sending feedback never does. Settings keeps its Rate row deep-linking to `?action=write-review`. No pre-prompt, enjoyment question or routing.

The app has no analytics capture call, so `rating_asked` is not logged.

## Remote config notes

- The control arm is today's copy character for character: `Start <intro trial label>` (for example "Start 7-day free trial") on onboarding, the blurred gates and the trial sheet, and `Start Free Trial` on the plan picker. The trial length is still read from the real intro offer.
- `my` and `your` read "Start my free trial" and "Start your free trial" on the one-tap buttons, and "Start My Free Trial" and "Start Your Free Trial" on the plan picker (title case).
- Trial-ineligible wording ("Try StatScout+ for <price>", win-back "Restart StatScout+ for <price>", "Subscribe", "Unlock Lifetime") is untouched and ignores the arm (3.1.2).
- The nav-bar "Try Free" pill is a navigation affordance, not a trial button, and is unchanged.
- The app ships English only (no string catalog), so no translations were needed.
- `rating_prompt` set to `off` pauses the star prompt. A missing, non-string or other value leaves it on.
- Metadata is read from `offerings.current` only after a successful offering fetch; before that, or if it fails, every key reads as control.

## Frequency follow-up (Rollout R, 2026-11-25)

The goal is the number of ratings. The two moments from Rollout S were too rare for the 2nd or 3rd session:

| Moment | How often a typical user hits it |
|---|---|
| Following a third player (old) | Rare. Most users follow zero to two players in their first few sessions, so most never reached it. |
| StatScout+ member opens a comparison (kept) | Rare. Only paying members, a small share of installs. |
| Following a player, first follow counts (new) | Common. Following is the core action (the watchlist feeds the Trends tab), free, one tap on the profile star, and a typical user does it by their 2nd or 3rd session. |
